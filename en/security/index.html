Protection model

Layers of control, explicit limits.

Titan Box combines local controls to make unauthorized access harder. No application can independently eliminate every risk relating to a device, operating system, or user decision.

Local access and content

Encryption and local vault

The app is designed to protect vault content on the device. Practical effectiveness also depends on device integrity, updates and access practices.

PIN and biometrics

A PIN protects app access. Where supported and enabled, device biometrics can facilitate local unlocking; they do not replace device security.

Local recovery

The recovery protocol remains local. Keep your PIN and access information responsibly.

Backup

Backups are created when you initiate them. Protecting a backup password and destination remains your responsibility.

Control over deletion

Trash separates restoring items from permanently deleting them. Session destruction, self-destruction and destruction codes are different functions: a session action is not the same as a complete wipe. Destructive actions deserve care because they can affect local data or access material.

Features requiring deliberate choices

  • Intruder selfie: when enabled, it may use the camera to create a local image connected with a failed access attempt, according to your chosen configuration.
  • Chameleon mode: changes visible app appearance or access; it does not change operating-system protections.
  • Sharing, viewer and export: may create an interaction with other apps or destinations. Review the destination before confirming.
  • Temporary files: some operations may require local temporary files. Titan Box limits and cleans its own scopes where applicable, while flash storage and operating systems have physical and operational limits.

What Titan Box does not promise

It does not promise absolute protection against a compromised device, prolonged physical access, malware, system modification, copies made by others, or sharing/export choices. Keep Android updated, use a screen lock, avoid rooted or compromised devices, and do not share PINs, backup passwords or sensitive content.

If you suspect your device is compromised, avoid entering new data, review device lock settings and seek trusted technical assistance.
