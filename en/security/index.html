Protection model

Layers of control, explicit limits.

Titan Box combines local protections to make unauthorized access harder. No app can independently remove every risk of a device, its system or user choices.

Local access and vault

Encryption and access

Vault content is designed to be protected locally. PIN and compatible biometrics help control access; security also depends on device lock, integrity and updates.

Recovery and backup

The Recovery Protocol is local, with no Lazarus server. Encrypted backups are created manually; protect their password and destination.

Controls and limits

  • Intruder Selfie requires configuration and camera permission.
  • Chameleon Mode changes visible appearance; it does not replace Android security.
  • Sharing, viewer, export and clipboard can deliver content to another app or destination.
  • Sensitive temporary files are limited to Titan scopes and cleaned when applicable; physical secure erase of flash is not promised.
  • Destroy session differs from Self-Destruction and destruction code; review every destructive action before use.

Diagnostics and privacy

The app includes no Analytics or AD_ID. Firebase Crashlytics may send technical crash diagnostics. Do not include secrets in support communications.

What Titan Box does not promise

It does not promise absolute protection against compromised devices, malware, prolonged physical access, system modification, third-party copies or sharing choices. Keep Android updated, use screen lock and avoid sharing secrets.
