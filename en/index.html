Privacy by design

No Titan account required.

The main flow does not require a Titan account. The vault is primarily managed on-device; purchases are processed by Google Play and technical diagnostics may use Firebase Crashlytics.

Read the privacy policy
Sharing, copying or exporting content is your decision. Once delivered to another app, service or person, that destination applies its own controls and policies.
