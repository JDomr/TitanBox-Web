Titan Box support

Before writing to us, check these answers. To protect your privacy, do not email vault content, PINs, backup passwords, recovery codes or sensitive files.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes and files stored?

The main vault flow is designed to remain local on your device. Backups or exports leave that environment only when you initiate them.

Can I use Titan Box without an account?

Yes. Titan Box does not require a Titan account for its main flow.

What happens if I change phones?

Use a backup you created and keep the required password safe. Before migrating, verify the backup and ensure the new device is trusted.

How do purchases and subscriptions work?

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Availability, price, renewal and cancellation are displayed and managed according to Google Play.

What if a purchase is not recognized?

Check that you use the same Google Play account, use the in-app restore purchases option, and allow the device to query Google Play. If it persists, contact support without including sensitive data.

What does Trash do?

Trash lets you restore removed items before permanently deleting them. Permanent deletion is different and can be irreversible.

What happens when I share or export?

Sharing or exporting is your decision. Once content reaches another app, service or person, Titan Box does not control that destination.

Contact

For app issues, email info@titanapps.eu. Include app version, device model and a non-sensitive description of the problem. Do not send secrets, protected content or private files.
