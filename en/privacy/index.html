Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026. This policy describes processing related to Titan Box and this website.

Titan Box is a product of Titan Future Tech LLC, 8 The Green, Suite B, Dover, Delaware 19901, United States (“Titan”, “we”, “us”). For privacy matters: info@titanapps.eu.

1. Summary

Titan Box is designed so that primary vault content is managed locally on your device. A Titan account is not required for the main use. We do not include advertising, Google Analytics, or the advertising permission/AD_ID. This does not mean all data always stays on a device: Google Play purchases, technical diagnostics, a backup you create, and a sharing or export action can involve third parties or destinations you choose.

2. Data the app may process

Vault content and local configuration

Notes, selected files, organization metadata, settings, access preferences, themes, Trash state, and data required for operation are primarily processed on-device. PINs, passwords and content should not be sent to support by email.

Camera and intruder selfie

If you enable the relevant feature and grant permission, the camera may be used to create a local image related to an access attempt. The feature depends on your configuration and Android permission.

Clipboard, sharing and export

When you copy, share, open or export content, you initiate an interaction with the clipboard, another app or an external destination. The receiving app, person or service may apply its own privacy policy. Review the destination before confirming and avoid exporting sensitive content unless needed.

Backups and recovery

Backups are generated at your initiative and are saved to a destination you select. Titan Box recovery is local. Protection, retention and deletion of a backup you choose also depend on that destination.

Purchase information

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play. Titan Box queries the purchase state needed to recognize access in the app. Google may process transaction, account and device information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Technical diagnostics

The app includes Firebase Crashlytics components to diagnose technical failures. Those components may process technical data such as app information, version, device, operating system, error state and installation data needed for diagnosis. We do not use Google Analytics or AD_ID for advertising. Avoid including sensitive vault information in error or support communications.

3. tbox.one website

This phase of the website is static: it contains no forms, analytics, trackers, marketing cookies, remote fonts or third-party CDNs. The contact link opens your email client. A hosting provider may process standard technical access logs once the site is published; that configuration and any additional notice will be reviewed before deployment.

4. Purposes and applicable basis

We use the described data to provide requested functionality, protect and maintain app operation, recognize Google Play purchases, respond to support requests and diagnose failures. Where applicable law requires a specific basis, it may include performance of a requested service, legitimate interests in security and maintenance, legal obligations, or consent for Android permissions and user-initiated actions.

5. Recipients and transfers

Depending on the feature, recipients may include Google Play for purchases and Firebase/Google for crash diagnosis. When you share, export or select a backup destination, you choose an additional recipient. Providers may operate infrastructure in different countries; their processing is governed by their own terms and applicable transfer mechanisms.

6. Retention and control

Local content remains on the device until it is removed through available functions, restore, uninstallation, or a destructive flow you choose. Exported backups and shared content are also governed by the chosen destination. Crashlytics technical data is retained under the provider’s configuration. You can manage permissions in Android and purchases/subscriptions in Google Play.

7. Security and limits

We use the local technical controls described on the Security page, but no system can promise absolute security. Protection also depends on device lock, updates, system integrity, passwords, export choices and physical control of the phone.

8. Rights and contact

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability or complaint to a competent authority. To ask about data Titan may process outside the device, email info@titanapps.eu. Do not send PINs, passwords, recovery codes or vault content. Local data can be managed directly from your device.

9. Children and changes

Titan Box is not specifically directed to children. We may update this policy if the app, site or applicable requirements change. We will publish the current version on this page with its update date.
